## `tables.json` — what it can and cannot show

`tests/fixtures/tables.json` pins **table identity** (heading + column names, via
`conftest.table_inventory`), not cell values. So the value movement the brief anticipated cannot
appear in it; it appears in the in-memory diff below instead.

Regenerated with `UPDATE_TABLE_FIXTURE=1`. The diff is **two deletions**: the
`["Favor & Recompense", ["Planet", "Condition", "Favored By"]]` table disappears from
`1240-05-26` in both `configurations/Abu Ma'shar (supplement)` and `configurations/Both`.

**Attributed to the fix**, mechanically: on 1240-05-26 the Sun stands at Gemini 12.03° (degree 13,
a Well degree). Under the transposed table its term lord was Jupiter (12–17); under Abū Ma'shar's
connection rule Jupiter is the only planet connected to the Sun (Sun–Venus sextile and Sun–Mars
conjunction are not connected under that profile). `evaluate_favor_and_recompense` requires the
helper to be a dispositor of the helped planet's degree, so "Sun (Well), favored by Jupiter" existed
only because of the wrong term lord. With Venus as the correct term lord (12–17) and Venus not
connected, there is no row, and `_finding()` suppresses the empty table.

**Consequence for the harness:** 1240-05-26 was chosen ("Reflection of Light and Favor &
Recompense") to exercise that table, and no longer does. Not changed here — it is a harness-design
decision. If wanted, 205 dates in 1240 (Florence, 14:30 LMT) render the table under the corrected
table; the earliest: 1240-01-04 (Sun, Well, by Saturn), 1240-01-05 (Venus, Well, by Jupiter),
1240-01-22 (Saturn, Well, by Jupiter).

The compromised run in between (suite started, then the fixture regenerated mid-run) failed exactly
those two 05-26 slots and nothing else — the fixture test working as designed.
