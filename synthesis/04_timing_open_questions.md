## 2. The open questions, by technique

### 2A. Which releaser, chosen how

Recorded statements:

- *Nat.* 1.15, 6–14 (Nawbakht): by day Sun in **1, 10, 11, 7, 8**, fit if one of its five lords aspects; else the meeting in those places; else the Ascendant. By night Moon → fullness → Fortune, each "in a stake or what follows a stake" with a lord aspecting. ¶13: *"that one … which is looking at the releaser, is the house-master."*
- *Times* 4, 2–3: Ascendant, its lord, the luminaries, Fortune and its lord, the prenatal syzygy — *"the stronger of these indicators … it is the victor and the indicator of the time."*
- *Nat.* 1.15, 5: Sahl **tested and rejected** Dorotheus's rule that a releaser in the seventh in a feminine sign is unfit.
- *Nat.* 1.16 (al-Andarzaghar) and 1.30 (Ptolemy) give further lists, recorded in `01_on_nativities.md` §1 only by pointer.

Questions for PN IV:
1. Is the release-by-list (1.15) or the release-by-victor (*Times* 4, 3) the natal rule, and do the two agree when applied to one chart?
2. **The eighth place** is in Nawbakht's day list; is it in PN IV's?
3. When the Sun is in a listed place but no lord aspects, and the meeting is also unfit — is the Ascendant fallback (1.15, 9) also PN IV's?
4. What does "in a stake or what follows a stake" mean for the Moon's fitness — whole sign, or the five-degree dynamic sense of 1.18, 19?

### 2B. The house-master: which lord, which years, gated by what

Recorded:

- Ranking *Nat.* 1.20, 2: **bound > house > exaltation > triplicity > image**; ¶3 two shares beat one; ¶4 the bound lord bodily with the releaser in the Ascendant beats all; ¶5 under the rays "deceptive, subtractive, corrupting". (*Questions* Ch. 13, 7 ranks house > triplicity > bound > face in another context — `01_on_questions.md` §3.)
- Where the years are granted, **two statements**:
  - *Nat.* 1.20, 10–12: greater years *"in the Ascendant or in the Midheaven, or in the sign of the west, or the eleventh, enhanced"*; also *"under the earth, eastern, in one of its shares"*; also *"in the house of Good Fortune and it is a diurnal planet in one of its places, eastern"*. ¶13 partly illegible.
  - *Times* 4, 7: *"if the ruler was in a stake, eastern, it grants its greater years; or if it was in what follows the stakes, it grants its middle years; and if it was falling, it grants its lesser years."*
- The solar-phase gate, VII.2, 12 (superiors past burning, under the rays, easternizing: *"suitable for granting their greater years as well as spear-bearing"*); VII.2, 32 (setting side, under the rays: *"not suitable for granting their greater years"*); VII.2, 40 (inferiors, the same after 7°). *Nat.* 1.22, 1 and 4: fitness *"in the distributing of the lifespan"* by the 6°/9-day and 22°/7-day allowances.
- Modifiers: *Nat.* 1.21, 11–12 — the Head with a fortunate house-master **adds one-fourth**; the Tail within **12°** subtracts one-fourth (fn. 169). 1.21 generally: aspects of the fortunes add, the infortunes subtract (recorded by pointer).
- Numbers: VII.8 Figure 146 (all cells verified against ¶3–8, checksum 75); *Times* Fig. 47 is wrong for Mars (18 for 15, Ch. 11, 39) and Mercury (`5, 10` for `5, 3`, Ch. 3, 16).
- Conversion: *Times* 11, 24: the lesser years *"in months or years"* — years when strong and received, months when weak; 11, 16: halved in a convertible sign.

Questions for PN IV:
5. Is 1.20's placement list (1, 10, 7, 11 for the greater years; the 4th/5th by night) the same rule as *Times* 4, 7's angular/succedent/cadent, or a different one? They cannot both be literal: 1.20, 10 gives the greater years to the **eleventh**, which *Times* 4, 7 calls succedent (middle years).
6. Does the solar-phase gate (VII.2) veto the grant outright, or reduce it a grade?
7. Do the fortunes' aspects (1.21) add a fixed quantity, a fraction, or the aspecting planet's own lesser years?
8. When the house-master is "enhanced" on one test and failing on another (e.g. angular but western), which grade?
9. Are the "mighty years" (VII.8, 1461 for the Sun) ever granted to a nativity, and on what condition?

### 2C. Distributions (directing through the bounds)

Recorded:

- Rates, *Nat.* 1.18, 21: *"for each degree (of the degrees of ascensions) a year, and for every 5′ a month, and for every 1′ six days, and for every 10″ a day"* — **by the ascensions of the signs in that city**. *Times* 4, 4 the same: *"a year for every degree by the ascensions of the signs in that city."*
- The distributor is *"the lord of <the bound of> that releaser"* (1.18, 20); the partner is *"the one casting its rays upon it"*; a bound ends the distribution and the next lord takes over (1.18, 22).
- The *jārbakhtār* (glossary; *Nat.* 11.3, 1 fn. 40) is the distributor; *Nat.* 11.3, 2 uses it as the timer of enmity: *"if you found the distributor of life to be an infortune … and you saw it in the Ascendant of your friend's nativity at the revolution of years."*
- 1.23, 34: the distributor rules *"many years according to [the size of] its bound (and the lord of the rays is like that)"*; 1.23, 37: *"an infortune corrupts four signs, unless it is received"*; 1.23, 60: the Head strengthens the governor, the Tail brings illness.
- 2.13, 48–51: the three 15° **ascensional** bands past a stake (and *Aphorisms* #45 must be read ascensionally — decided policy).
- 4.12, 5 (al-Khayyāt): *"let your directing be by the bounds and not distributed, but direct them to the infortunes and the fortunes"* (fn. 175: *"this is strange"*); 4.12, 6: a retrograde planet's rays are directed conversely.
- The engine's Timing page uses a flat 1° of zodiacal longitude per year and labels it as *not* this (`app.py` coverage entry for 1.18, 20–22); the ascension tables VII.7 presupposes are not in the corpus (VII.7 fns. 250–251).

Questions for PN IV:
10. Ascensions of the **city** (oblique) throughout, or right ascensions for a planet near the meridian, as VII.7's ray-casting distinguishes by quadrant?
11. What is "reaching": the body only, or rays too, and with what orb — 1.18, 20 says *"the one casting its rays upon it"*; VII.5 fn. 146 says rays have no orb.
12. Does "an infortune corrupts four signs" (1.23, 37) mean four whole signs of the direction, or four bounds?
13. Is the Ascendant the only point distributed, or the releaser, or several (*Nat.* 3.9, 23 directs a Lot *"in the bounds of all of the signs, just as it is directed for the Moon"*; 4.20, 32–37 directs the Sun and the Lot of the father)?
14. How does the distributor's own natal condition (received, in the rays, retrograde) modify the period?

### 2D. Profections and the lord of the year

Recorded:

- *Nat.* 1.24, 1: *"starting out from the Ascendant is a year or a month for every sign; then it returns every twelve months to the Ascendant, and every twelve years to the Ascendant."* ¶3: when the turning reaches a fortune or infortune it takes that nature, *"except that the lord of the year alters the condition by itself."*
- ⚠ **1.23, 33 vs 1.24, 2** — the distributor is stronger (the shepherd and the hireling) vs *"turning … is stronger <than> the distributor of time"*; fn. 245: *"This should probably read that it is stronger when combined with the distributor."* Unresolved.
- 7.4, 4: *"if the year made the rounds from the nativity, so that it reached the sign in which the Lot of wedding was, and Saturn is not looking, then he will marry."* 5.3, 3: *"when the year reaches the sign in the root of the nativity in which Jupiter and Venus [each] are."*
- 11.1, 43 (Dorotheus): make the root of a friendship *"when the Moon is in the sign which is the Ascendant of the year in the revolution of years"*; fn. 25: the Greek has the native's *profected* Ascendant on the other's natal Moon, and Sahl's source offers the revolution Ascendant as a more frequent substitute.
- 3.2, 73 and 3.10, 11: the lord of the third *"from the Ascendant of the year"* in the tenth — the houses of the year chart read topically.

Questions for PN IV:
15. Which points are profected — the Ascendant only, or the releaser, the Lots, each house?
16. Monthly profections: a sign per month from the year's sign, or from the Ascendant, and starting when?
17. How the lord of the year is judged: by its natal condition, its revolution condition, or both; and its precedence over the distributor (1.23, 33 / 1.24, 2).
18. Is *"the Ascendant of the year"* (11.1, 43; 3.2, 73) the profected sign or the revolution chart's Ascendant? The text uses it both ways.

### 2E. Solar revolutions — the whole apparatus is absent

Recorded, all pointers:

- 1.15, 4: an obstacle *"perhaps … in the revolution of years, and perhaps … in the transit of the planets, and perhaps … due to the direction of the releaser."*
- 4.1, 3: *"fathers do not have a revolution in nativities, nor do cities, nor does anything not initiating a revolution; but certainly a revolution and change-over does belong to everything one does initiate"* — with fn. 1's long discussion of whose chart is revolved.
- 4.20, 47: *"revolve the year"* when the infortunes correspond to the Lot's position; 4.20, 52: *"the year reached the sign in which there was an infortune in the root … or the Ascendant of the revolution was in one of the stakes of the nativity."*
- 10.2.6, 49–56: the "corrupted or suitable" circle at birth *"grants the rank which he was born at"*; *"whenever the circle returns to corruption, his condition is corrupted"* (fn. 186 reads this as revolutions or distributions).
- 4.20, 25: *"especially if Jupiter was the lord of the year of the nativity, and he is in a stake, eastern."*
- 3.8, 4: *"if in the transformation of the year Mercury was with the infortunes, it kills his siblings."*

Questions for PN IV:
19. How the revolution is cast (the Sun's return to its natal degree; which Ascendant), and whether its houses are read topically as 3.2, 73 implies.
20. The relation of the revolution's Ascendant to the natal stakes (4.20, 52) — a named condition; what else in the revolution chart is compared to the root?
21. The "lord of the year" — is it the profection lord, the revolution's Ascendant lord, or a victor?
22. Whether revolutions of the *month* and *day* exist in PN IV, as the month-per-sign of 1.24, 1 suggests.

### 2F. Transits combined with directions

Recorded:

- 4.13, 2 / 4.20, 38–41: when the Lot of the father's bound lord and house lord are both corrupt, the father dies *"when Saturn passes over it in his transiting, before he casts his light upon it … (and likewise if he cast his light upon it before he goes past it)"*; if both are sound, only when *"one of the two infortunes casts its light upon it—unless Venus or Jupiter casts the light equally into the bounds."* fn. 179: a combination of directions and transits.
- 4.20, 28–30 (Dorotheus): the infortunes transiting the natal luminaries' signs, or the Lot from the IC, kill the parents; *"he does not omit the revolution of years and the transit of the [malefic] planets over the Moon and Sun."*
- 4.20, 31: the malefics that harm the father's Lot — Mars and Saturn by day (and Mercury if unfortunate), **Mars and Mercury by night**, because Saturn is the father's significator by night (fn. 288).
- 4.20, 33–35: two infortunes casting rays on the Sun and the Lot — *"grant that one the power"* which is harsher in the root; ties to the one the luminaries are closer to.
- 5.3, 8: *"when Jupiter reaches this Lot [Mars→Jupiter] in his course and transit, or looks at it from an opposition or square, then one will be born to him in that year."* 7.4, 1–3, 5–8: Jupiter's transit to the Lot of wedding, to Venus's natal sign or its lord, to the Sun→Moon Lot; Saturn's to his own natal sign or the Lot.
- 4.3, 16–17: directing the father's indicator *"to the light of Mars, for every degree of it a month; and if it went beyond that, then make it years"*; if Mars is powerful, *"make it days when you direct that degree through the bounds."*
- 6.5, 1–3: the timing of chronic illness by quadrant of the indicator, by easternness, and by the lord of the sixth's house.

Questions for PN IV:
23. Whether a transit "before the ray" and a direction "before the transit" are two triggers that both fire, or a single window.
24. The orb of a transit over a Lot or a natal degree — by sign (4.20, 28) or by degree.
25. Whether a transit is read only in the year the distributor or profection also points there (11.3, 2 reads that way).
26. The unit switch in 4.3, 16–17 (month → year → day by the malefic's power): is it PN IV's, or the Bizidaj's alone?

### 2G. *Fardārs*

Recorded: VII.8, 3 — Sun 10, Venus 8, Mercury 13, Moon 9, Saturn 11, Jupiter 12, Mars 7, Head 3, Tail 2, *"that is 75 years"*; VII.8, 2 — *"as for the reasons for them, we have stated them in the book in which it is necessary to mention them."* Nothing else in the corpus mentions the *fardār*.

Questions for PN IV:
27. The order of the lords by day and by night (the Sun-first / Moon-first sequences), and where the Nodes sit.
28. The sub-period division (each *fardār* into seven, in what order) and whether the Nodes have sub-periods.
29. Whether the *fardār* lord is judged from the natal chart or from each revolution.

### 2H. The remaining named methods

- **Ages by quadrant**: *Nat.* 3.9, 32–36 — *"they are young until they reach the Midheaven, and there they will be sons of 26 years … the west … 42 years … under the earth as old men … 82 years."* 2.22 [2] names the method. Question 30: is this PN IV's ages-of-life scheme, or Ptolemy's seven ages (1.3, Fig. 48)?
- **Valens's Fortune-years**: 1.37, 35–36 and 6.5, 4–6 give **different** numbers for the same aspects and signs (opposition 7 in both; but right/left trine and square 9/10/3 vs 3/11/4/10/5/9; Aries 19 vs 15). Question 31: which table, if either, does PN IV use?
- **Ascensions and periods** (2.22 [4]): question 32 — is this method in PN IV at all?
- **Repelling years** (2.7, 1–7): Jupiter in a stake repels evil *"for twelve years"*, Venus eight, Saturn (as triplicity lord) thirty, Mercury twenty, Mars fifteen, the Sun nineteen by day, the Moon twenty-five in a female sign *"and if she was in a male sign, those years will be months"* — the lesser years again, with the same years/months switch as *Times* 11. Question 33: is the years/months conversion by sign gender (2.7, 7), by strength (*Times* 11, 24), or by quadruplicity (*Times* 11, 16)?
- **Assets timing** (2.7, 12): from the Lot of assets to the lord of the second *"by degrees of ascensions"*. Siblings' death (3.8, 3): *"a year for every degree, by degrees of ascensions."*
- **Meeting / opposition nativities** (8.7, 2): *"nativities of the meeting will die at the opposition, and nativities of the opposition will die on the meeting"*; 8.7, 3–4: death on the weekday of the lord of the fourth hour from birth. Question 34: are these in PN IV or only here?
- **Marriage age by phase and quadrant** (7.4, 9–17): Venus eastern → young, western → old; Ptolemy's quadrants of the Sun/Moon.

## 4. What a builder needs from PN IV before anything is queued

In priority order, each tied to the questions above:

1. **The revolution chart itself** — how cast, how read, its relation to the root (Q19–22). Nothing in the corpus supplies this.
2. **The single releaser/house-master procedure PN IV actually uses**, so that *Nat.* 1.15/1.20 and *Times* 4 can be reconciled or one chosen (Q1–9).
3. **The distributor's rules** — rays and orbs, the "four signs", the several points directed (Q10–14) — and **ascension tables** for the locality (VII.7 fns. 250–251), which are computable but not in the corpus.
4. **Profection scope and the lord of the year** (Q15–18), which decides disagreement #1.
5. **The *fardār* sequence** (Q27–29), which nothing in the corpus even hints at.
6. **The time-unit keys** (Q33, disagreements #5–6), because every per-topic timing rule in *On Nativities* depends on one.

Until then, the Timing page stays as it is: a labelled shortcut (1°/year zodiacal), with the
ascensional method named as the real one and not implemented.

## 5. What is already safe to rely on (recorded, still not queued)

- The planetary-years numbers: Figure 146 (VII.8), verified cell by cell and by checksum.
- The distribution **rates** (1.18, 21) and the requirement that they be **ascensional**.
- The lesser-years-as-years-or-months conversion (*Times* 11, 24) — with its three rival triggers
  noted (#5).
- The solar-phase gate on granting the greater years (VII.2, 12, 32, 40).
- The Head/Tail quarter-adjustment to the house-master's years (1.21, 11–12, Tail within 12°).
- The five-degree rule as the boundary of a stake for dynamic placements (1.18, 19; 1.22, 9;
  Aph. #44), and the ascensional reading of the 15° bands (2.13, 48–51; Aph. #45 policy).
