## ⚠ 0. The scope finding, stated up front

*On Times* is a **horary timing** work — "all of the times **in questions**" (Ch. 3, 8). It is
Sahl's, not Abū Ma'shar's *Revolutions*, so it does not lift the timing deferral.

**But Chs. 4 and 11 contain natal longevity and years-granting procedure**, not merely question
timing, and that changes one claim I made earlier. See §4 and §7 — this is the most consequential
result of reading the omitted chapters.

## 1. What a "time" is (Ch. 1, 2–7)

> "Know that times vary according to the variety of motions… **6** the **alternation of figures and
> the changing of [their] conditions** in the celestial circle are all indicative for what they are
> consistent with, of good or evil." — Times Ch. 1, 2–6

Times are read from *changes of condition*: shifts in longitude, in latitude, and the daily rotation
(¶7).

## 2. ⚠ Two named schemes for the quick/slow (advancing/retreating) division — Sahl picks Māshā'allāh's

The single most important doctrinal item in the work, because it bears on the project's open
advancing question.

**Scheme A — "the ancients": quadrants.**

> "a planet is in what is **between the Ascendant and the Midheaven**, and what is opposite that
> (and that is what is **between the setting and the stake of the earth**): and this position of the
> circle indicates **quickness**. **11** And their westernness is that a planet is … between the
> stake of the fourth and the Ascendant, and what is opposite that … **slowness and delay,
> according to the statement of the ancients**." — Times Ch. 1, 10–11

**Scheme B — Māshā'allāh: hemispheres.**

> "as for Māshā'allāh, indeed **he differs from them** … he makes the quick place of the circle be
> what begins its rise … (and it is what is **from the degree of the fourth**), [then] turned back
> **towards the Midheaven** … **13** Then, what is **between the Midheaven and the fourth** is called
> 'turned back to the slow side, sloping…'" — Times Ch. 1, 12–13

**Sahl endorses B:**

> "**14** And that is **the closest of the two statements**, in [its] analogy." — Times Ch. 1, 14

(fn. 7: *"That is, most appropriate, most resembling the truth."*)

**Figure 44 (p. 233) shows both and agrees with the prose.** Read directly: left wheel = quadrants
(ASC→MC and DSC→IC quick, the other two slow); right wheel = hemispheres, with a dashed arrow
running IC→ASC→MC marked **Quick** and the descending half marked **Slow**.

⚠ **And the hemisphere scheme is attested a third time, operationally, in another work** —
*On Choices* Ch. 6, 16–17 uses exactly this division for medical elections:

> "treat every pain from the head to the navel when the Moon is in what is between the stake of the
> earth, so **rising up to the Midheaven** … and it is the place which is called **the highest region
> of the circle**. **17** And if the malady was from the navel to the bottom of the foot, then treat
> it when the Moon is in what is between the **tenth, declining towards the stake of the earth**: and
> that is **the lowest part of the circle**."

So the rising/descending hemisphere division is not a stray remark in a timing chapter — Sahl **uses
it** in a second work. That strengthens it considerably over what I could say from Ch. 1 alone.

**Caveat that must travel with this:** Ch. 1's subject is *quickness of timing* and *On Choices*
Ch. 6's is a bodily correspondence. Neither says the hemisphere division governs
*advancing-as-strength*. The vocabulary and the geometry are shared; the application is not stated.

## 3. Sign type and place → time unit (Ch. 2)

> "the signs of the quick planets as well as the **convertible** signs are, in times, the quick ones:
> **days and months**. **3** And the signs which belong to the heavy planets, and the **fixed** signs,
> are **years and months**. **4** … if the indicator of the time was in the fixed signs, it indicates
> **years**, and if it was in the **embodied** signs they indicate **months**, and if it was in the
> convertible signs it indicates **days**." — Times Ch. 2, 2–4

> "**7** And if the planetary indicator of the time was in an embodied sign, then **double the
> time**." — Times Ch. 2, 7

**Figure 46 agrees with the prose.** By place:

> "the **Ascendant and Midheaven** both indicate **quickness**… the **seventh** indicates **months**
> (and that is slower), and the **stake of the earth** retards and indicates **years**."
> — Times Ch. 2, 6

⚠ **Editorial subtraction:** fns. 19–20 record that Dykes **omitted** two parenthetical clauses
present only in **B** and **Es**, judging them redundant and unjustified. Recorded.

## 4. ★ Ch. 4 — the releaser, directions by ascensions, and the greater/middle/lesser years rule

**This corrects a claim I made in `01_abu_mashar_vii_7_to_9.md` §D2**, where I said the planetary
years' *values* were present but the *procedure* for granting them was missing and awaited PN4.
A procedure is present, here:

> "look at the **Ascendant** … and its **lord**, and the **luminaries**, and the **Lot of Fortune and
> its lord**, and the **meeting or opposition which was before the question**. **3** Take the
> **stronger of these indicators**, and the one of them greater in testimony, for it is **the victor
> and the indicator of the time**." — Times Ch. 4, 2–3

That is the standard five-candidate releaser set, chosen by victor — matching the glossary's
*Releaser* entry (p. 790) almost exactly.

> "**4** **Direct it just like you direct the [longevity] releaser** to the assembly of the infortunes
> and their aspect, **a year for every degree by the ascensions of the signs in that city**. **5** Now
> if the fortunes were not casting rays to that bound, the questioner will die in that year. **6** And
> the more forceful rays [of the infortunes] … are the **square and opposition**." — Times Ch. 4, 4–6

Directing by **oblique ascensions for the locality**, a year per degree — i.e. the ascensional
version of directions, which is exactly what `NOT_IMPLEMENTED_COVERAGE` records as absent for
*On Nativities* Ch. 1.18, 20–22.

**And then the years-granting rule:**

> "**7** Now if the victor was received, or if the releaser was [itself] ruling, then **if the ruler
> was in a stake, eastern, it grants its greater years; or if it was in what follows the stakes, it
> grants its middle years; and if it was falling, it grants its lesser years**." — Times Ch. 4, 7

**Angular → greater · succedent → middle · cadent → lesser.** This is the house-master
(*alcocoden*) grant, and the corpus now holds both halves: the rule here, and the four-column
numbers in Abū Ma'shar's Figure 146 (VII.8).

**Māshā'allāh's alternative method** follows immediately, and it is different:

> "**8** And Māshā'allāh said: Look at the lord of the Ascendant and the Moon, for indeed **the
> lifespan is from the lord of the Ascendant, and calamities are from the Moon**. **9** So if they
> both (or one of them) connected with the infortunes or burning, then know the amount of the
> degrees which are between it and the infortune or burning… **10** Now if the lord of the Ascendant
> was in a **fixed** sign, it is a **year** for every degree; and if … **two bodies**, a **month**;
> and if … **convertible**, a **day**." — Times Ch. 4, 8–10

⚠ **Two competing longevity procedures in one chapter, both named**, and Sahl does not adjudicate
between them. ⚠ And this is a **question** chart's lifespan, not a nativity's — though ¶4's "just
like you direct the [longevity] releaser" presupposes the natal technique rather than defining it.

**Do not read this as lifting the deferral.** What it changes is narrower and worth stating exactly:
the *granting rule* and the *releaser candidate set* are in the corpus. The full natal apparatus —
which releaser wins under which sect conditions, the house-master's own qualification tests, and the
revolution techniques that modify the result — is not.

## 5. Chs. 5–7 — brief per-house timing

- **Assets** (Ch. 5, 2–3): degrees between the two significators; or the ingress of the lord of the
  2nd into the Ascendant, or the lord of the Ascendant into the 2nd. ¶4 notes the 3rd and 4th are
  handled elsewhere.
- **Children** (Ch. 6, 2–3): from the lord of the 5th to the 5th — "**a month for every sign and a
  day for every degree**."
- **Illness** (Ch. 7): "**Do not omit to call upon the Moon as a witness in all of the sick person's
  states**" (¶2). Direct the Moon to the infortune's degree and to the lord of the 8th; **Saturn: a
  month or year per degree; Mars: a day or month** (¶6). "And do not neglect the degree of burning"
  (¶7).

## 6. Ch. 8 — wars, from Theophilus and Hermes

Explicitly attributed: "**From the statement of Theophilus**" (¶2), "**And Hermes said**" (¶17),
"this chapter is **from the secrets of the statement of Hermes**" (¶25). So Ch. 8 is transmission,
not Sahl's own composition — relevant before citing any of it as "Sahl says."

One item of general interest, because it uses machinery the engine has:

> "if the **twelfth-part of the Moon** was in the Ascendant or Midheaven, or the place of the Sun or
> the lord of his house … that indicates the quickness of the fighting." — Times Ch. 8, 6

A **use of twelfth-parts beyond the Moon's own** — `NOT_IMPLEMENTED_COVERAGE` records
"*Uses of the TWELFTH-PARTS beyond the Moon's*" as absent for *On Nativities*; this is the Moon's
twelfth-part, so that entry is unaffected, but the technique is corroborated in a second work.

Also ¶19–24: Saturn and Jupiter quick in course → quick victory; slow → drawn out; and the
quadruplicity of their sign governs the spread of the fighting.

## 7. ★ Ch. 11 — the years each planet grants, and the lesser-years link

Ch. 11 is *On Times*' longest chapter and is Māshā'allāh's, on the duration of a ruler's power.
Doctrinally it is a **time-lord and revolution** chapter — deferred — but ¶24–45 states a rule that
ties directly to the planetary-years tables:

> "**24** And look at the governing planets: if they were **in the stakes and they had strength**,
> they grant the amount of **their lesser years, in months or years**." — Times Ch. 11, 24

Then, planet by planet:

| Planet | Strong / received | Weak | Ch. 11 ¶ |
|---|---|---|---|
| ♄ in ASC/MC with testimony, received | **30 years** | 30 months | 25, 29 |
| ♃ in ASC/MC with testimony | **12 years** | — | 31 |
| ♀ in ASC/MC with testimony | 10 months; received & free of infortunes **8 years** | — | 32, 34 |
| ☿ in ASC/MC, testimony, received | **20 months** | 5 months | 36, 37 |
| ♂ in ASC/MC | **15 months** | — | 39 |
| ☽ received, free, with testimony | **25 years** | 25 months | 40 |
| ☉ accepting testimony | 1 year; great in testimony **19 years** | 19 months | 44, 45 |

**Every one of these is that planet's lesser-years number** (♄30 ♃12 ♂15 ☉19 ♀8 ☿20 ☽25), granted
as **years when strong and received, months when weak**. That is a clean, stated conversion rule,
and it is the doctrinal bridge between Figure 47/Figure 146's numbers and their use.

### 7a. ✅ This settles Figure 47's Mars discrepancy from inside the text

The earlier artifact flagged that Figure 47 gives Mars "18" while fn. 29 says *"This should
certainly be 15, as found in **Ch. 11, 39** below."* I have now read Ch. 11, 39, and it does say

the photograph.)

## 8. Chs. 9–10 — travel and reports

Ch. 9 (travel) and Ch. 10 (books and reports, "from [Māshā'allāh]") are topical. Two items of
general shape:

> "if you found the Moon in the Ascendant at the beginning of the journey, then indeed **his stay on
> the journey will be lengthened**" — Times Ch. 9, 7

> "if you were asked about a book, **how long ago it was written**, then look at the degrees which
> are between the Moon and the planet the Moon is **being separated from**" — Times Ch. 10, 7

The second is a **retrospective** use of separation — measuring elapsed time backwards from a
separating aspect — which is the mirror of the ordinary forward application. Worth noting because
it is a use of separation the engine's forward-looking machinery does not have.
